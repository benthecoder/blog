## ingredients

- 800 ml water
- 1 dashi bag (Katsuofuridashi)
- ~45 g red miso (Hanamaruki)
- ~90 g tofu, cubed
- a handful of dried wakame

## directions

- soak the wakame until soft, drain
- boil the water with the dashi bag a few minutes, take the bag out
- add tofu and wakame, warm through
- turn off the heat, stir in the miso
