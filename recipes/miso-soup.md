## ingredients

- 800 ml water
- 12 g kombu
- 24 g katsuobushi
- 1 tbsp sake
- ~45 g miso, 2 parts red to 1 part white
- 150 g tofu, cubed
- 1 tsp dried wakame
- scallions

## directions

- soak kombu in the water for 30 min (or overnight)
- heat slowly, pull the kombu out just before it boils
- bring to a boil, turn off heat, add katsuobushi, wait 2 min, strain without squeezing
- add sake, simmer 1 min
- add tofu and wakame, warm on low
- turn off heat, whisk in the miso through a ladle or sieve
- taste, add more miso a little at a time. never let it boil again
- top with scallions
