## ingredients

- 800 ml water
- 1 Katsuofuridashi dashi bag (dried bonito, herring, kelp — the bag kind, not the Hondashi powder; that one has lactose in it, weirdly)
- ~45 g Hanamaruki red miso (one heaped spoonful)
- ~90 g Wildwood super firm tofu, cubed
- a big handful of dried cut wakame — way too much. it became a whole plate once soaked; 1-2 g is plenty, it expands ~10x

## directions

- soak the wakame in water until soft, drain and squeeze
- boil the water with the dashi bag for a few minutes, take the bag out
- add tofu and wakame, warm through
- stir in the miso. as cooked: straight off the spoon into the boiling pot. next time take it off the boil first and dissolve the miso in a ladle of broth — boiling kills the fermented good stuff
- the leftover wakame keeps 3 days in the fridge, or freeze it in portions and drop it frozen into the next soup
