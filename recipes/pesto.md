## ingredients

- ~60 g basil leaves
- ~20 g pine nuts
- 2 garlic cloves
- juice of 1/2 lemon
- 3 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 2 tsp red miso
- ~1 tsp salt
- ~80 ml olive oil

## directions

- toast the pine nuts in a dry pan until golden, set aside to cool
- blanch the basil in boiling salted water for 30 seconds, straight into ice water, squeeze dry
- food processor: pine nuts, garlic and salt, pulse to coarse crumbs
- add the basil, lemon juice and miso, pulse while drizzling in the olive oil
- stir in the nutritional yeast by hand, taste for salt and lemon
