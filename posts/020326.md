my stuff finally arrived. it was delayed for a week, and i had to cycle through different hotels and Airbnbs. my movers were an interesting combo: a tall older white man and a shorter African-American man. one was large and slow, the other small but swift. it was almost comedic. they did not seem to know each other well, and seemed to have been assigned at random. i spoke with the older man, sharing about how i went to school in iowa, and he had parents or grandparents who went to iowa as well, i can't remember the details. but he was interested in malaysia and was curious to learn more about my culture.

the entire process took less than two hours, and everything seemed to arrive in one piece. i had no complaints, except that i wished it had arrived on time as promised. i even paid extra for it to arrive on time, so you can imagine how dissatisfied i was. of course i called and emailed a bunch of times to get compensation.

it's weird to think about how this place will be my home for the foreseeable future. moving into a new apartment alone, without having seen it in person, doesn't quite feel like moving in. it feels like a temporary job relocation. i didn't personally choose to live here, and it feels like an extended work trip.
