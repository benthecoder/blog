- how dal works
  - lentils: the base, gives texture and body
  - aromatics (onion, garlic, ginger): the savory foundation
  - spices (cumin, turmeric, coriander or curry powder, chili, garam masala to finish): define the flavor
  - fat (oil, ghee, coconut milk): carries flavor and richness
  - liquid (water or broth): cooks the lentils creamy
  - acid (tomatoes, lemon, tamarind): balances the heaviness
  - tadka (hot oil and spices poured at the end): a concentrated flavor boost
  - mental model: lentils = body, aromatics + spices = identity, fat + tadka = why it tastes good
