there was a mini party in our apartment today hosted by a church friend who coincidentally lived in the same apartment as i did. as a fun bonding activity, and also for our own consumption purposes, we decided to bake cookies. we dumped all the healthy and boring ingredients we had (cacao nibs, hemp seeds, oats, banana, vanilla essence, honey, etc.) and measured everything by gut. we also had claude direct us throughout the process, and the outcome was not terrible.

it came out more soft and chewy than the crispy and crunchy cookies i commonly see, i think those cookies have the help of a ton of sugar to help crystallize them. but either way people enjoyed it and liked that it was healthy. i also somehow met three medical students, and i shared about OE. we had conversations about language, best directors, about miami, and career.

at the end we helped pack up, took some leftovers, and when we reached home we were so tired we crashed out on the floor till 1 a.m.

in the morning T flies out on a very early flight, and our plan was to stay awake all night, but i ended up passing out continuously on the floor, while she packed to go home. my body was not strong enough. maybe i slept so that i would think it's all a bad dream, and that she won't be leaving me to go back to seattle.
