we visited the vizcaya museum. this is the first museum i've been to in florida, and it reminded me of the isabella stewart gardner museum in boston, but miami edition.

from some brief research with claude, apparently it was built between 1914 and 1922 as the winter home of james deering, who made his money selling farm machines. adjusting for inflation, it cost roughly $450–600m in today's terms.

he basically hired over 1,100 masons and craftsmen, most of them from europe, and to make it look old and passed down, he intentionally distressed the stones and filled it with genuine antiques from the 15th to 19th centuries, and planted mature trees in the garden. there's also a large stone ship called the barge sitting in the bay which acts as a breakwater.

he died in 1925, the 1926 hurricane tore the place up, and it opened as a museum in 1953.
