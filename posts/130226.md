today is the day. my one way ticket from aus -> mia. austin gave me a proper send off by appointing a cuban man as my uber driver, as he recommends me some places like La Carreta and El Palacio De Los Jugos. words that do not mean anything to me at the moment, but probably will in a few weeks.

at the airport i encountered the longest queue ever. 

![austin airport](/images/130226-aus.jpg)

i'm glad i got here early. i still had time to work at airport for an hour before i boarded my flight.

landing down in Miami, i kept replaying the conversation i had with R when we were last here visiting the office, that i would never move here. funny how some things are out of our control, perhaps there's a greater reason for me being here. i'll only find out later in my life.
