- Hepatitis B core antibody
- Hepatitis B surface antibody
- Hepatitis C antibody

![blood tubes](/images/110326-img-8725.jpg)

- the tubes are sealed with a vacuum inside so that when a needle enters a vein, the negative pressure automatically pulls the exact right amount of blood into the tube.
- the different colors indicate which additive is inside to keep the blood from clotting or to preserve it for specific types of tests
  - lavender / purple: contains EDTA; used for complete blood counts (CBC)
  - light blue: contains sodium citrate; used for blood clotting and coagulation studies.
  - gold (SST): contains a clot activator and gel; used for chemistry panels, hormones, and lipid profiles.
  - green: contains heparin; used for rapid chemical tests or certain drug monitoring levels
  - gray: contains sodium fluoride/potassium oxalate; used for glucose or blood alcohol testing.
