- [Everything I know about good system design](https://www.seangoedecke.com/good-system-design)
  - good system design looks underwhelming, bad design is often more impressive than good. a complex system usually reflects an absence of good design.
  - state is the hardest part. minimize the number of stateful components in any system
  - database is the most important component for managing state. make indexes match the most common queries you send. each index adds write overhead.
  - transactions and writes overload the DB, beware of spikes of queries (e.g. bulk import API), throttle your queries.
