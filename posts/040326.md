some notes on gospel-centered community from the New to Grace group at Grace Church.

there are two aspects of being honest with people: telling the truth about ourselves and telling the truth about others.

here's a small exercise: think of something you don't want other people to know.

now identify the idol: why do you want to keep it hidden?
- approval: would people still like me if they found out?
- control: can i still control how others perceive me if they found out?
- respect: would they still respect me if they knew the truth?
- reputation: what would others do with that information?

look to Jesus for an example of how he overcame these idols and how you can seek your new identity in the gospel.
