> What the hell is good design? Is it something that gives satisfaction to your own ego? Or is it something that creates a positive response in the eyes, minds, and hearts of millions of people? Also, is it something that is appropriate to its category? Ideally it is all of these.
> — Walter Landor

walter landor was born [walter landauer](https://www.veroniquevienne.com/article/walter-landor_-the-captain-of-the-klamath) in munich in 1913 and changed his name in 1931 after seeing a london street named for the writer walter savage landor. he started his firm in san francisco in 1941.

in 1964 he bought a retired ferryboat called [the klamath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Landor) for $12,000 and moved the whole office onto it. it had a supermarket mockup and a photo studio, and the friday night parties reportedly drew people like [andy warhol and the grateful dead](https://www.veroniquevienne.com/article/walter-landor_-the-captain-of-the-klamath).

my favorite is muni's [worm logo](https://www.sfmta.com/blog/doing-worm-brief-history-munis-graphic-art) and the orange "sunset" paint scheme, which came out in 1975 on trolley coach 5001. before that muni used a round [S.F. badge](https://www.sfmta.com/blog/doing-worm-brief-history-munis-graphic-art) from 1919 to 1968, then a maroon ribbon logo. when the SFMTA was created in 1999 some people wanted to replace the worm with a new logo and the worm won.

some other work by the [firm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landor_Associates), not all of it by walter himself:

- [levi's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Landor) (late 60s), bank of america (1969), del monte (1965)
- sapporo (1959), coca-cola (1985), world wildlife fund (1986), and fedex
- airlines like japan airlines, british airways, alitalia, delta, cathay pacific and singapore airlines
- packaging for miller lite (1972), marlboro (1977), frito-lay (around 1980), dole (1984) and fujifilm (1987)
- names too, like delta's [skymiles](https://www.veroniquevienne.com/article/walter-landor_-the-captain-of-the-klamath) and at&t's [lucent](https://www.veroniquevienne.com/article/walter-landor_-the-captain-of-the-klamath)

he retired in 1989 and died in 1995. he reportedly wanted "[i couldn't have had a better time](https://www.veroniquevienne.com/article/walter-landor_-the-captain-of-the-klamath)" on his tombstone.
