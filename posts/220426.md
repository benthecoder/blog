notes from a session on reading the bible:

- who's reading: a lost sinner (needs Jesus), or someone born again (believer, child of God, indwelt by the Spirit)
- goal: be confident reading it, so you can understand it, enjoy it, live in light of it, and help others understand (psalm 119:99)
- step 1: read
  - pray: a direct conversation with God
  - what are you seeing? (observation)
- step 2: understand
  - the author's intention: what did it mean for the writer?
  - the temptation is to jump straight to application
  - use other parts of the bible for context
  - 1 corinthians 2:14–16: you can't learn the truth apart from the Spirit of God
- step 3: worship
  - prepare your heart
- step 4: apply
  - scripture put into practice
