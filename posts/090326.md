i woke up and continued to have cramps. it got so bad that i decided to go to the baptist urgent care center.

they first measured my BP, which was 100/58.

then i waited for what felt like hours, until a doctor with a strong accent came in and spoke with me about my symptoms. he decided that we should do a CT scan to look at the lining of my intestines to check for any inflammation.

i waited another 30 minutes for the CT scan results.

the results came back showing i have slight thickening of my small intestine lining. the next step was to see a GI specialist, which could mean waiting months. that's healthcare in the US for you.

before i left, i joined a zoom call and talked to a virtual person to pay for my visit. i hadn't experienced that before. i thought it was quite cool.

![zoom call](/images/090326-img-8710.jpg)

that night i ate subpar pho that was extremely salty.

i miss home.
