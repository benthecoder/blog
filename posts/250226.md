![midtown dentist](/images/250226-midtown-dentist.jpg)

i scheduled an appt at midtown for a general cleaning. i have not done any cleaning since i started braces treatment and after taking them off. i felt nervous, what if i have hidden cavities? how can i know if they're real and my dentist was telling the truth. 

i waited an hour before i was called into the room. they did a full scan of my teeth again, even though i just did the same thing yesterday. 

it was some time before the actual dentist showed up. she was very efficient, picked on some of my teeth and started explaining i have some small cavities due to my braces treatment and my lack of flossing.

she seemed trustworthy and showed me what it would cost, and agreed. the entire treatment and procedure was pretty painless, although i was clueless on what was happening the entire time.

this was what i noted down while sitting on the chair.
- UL ANTIBACT IRR/QUAD
- UR ANTIBACT IRR/QUAD
- LL ANTIBACT IRR/QUAD
- LR ANTIBACT IRR/QUAD
- SCALING IN PRESENCE OF GENERALIZED MODERATE OR SEVERE GINGIVAL INFLAMMATION
- TOPICAL APPLICATION OF FLUORIDE VARNISH
- ORAL HYGIENE INSTRUCTION

i just know in the end i was charged 320 dollars for the cleaning, cavity filling and laser.

after leaving, i started looking into reddit and other sources about whether i was scammed. i read how dentists would scare you into useless procedures for insurance scams, and i felt hopeless. i wish i could go back Malaysia for things like these.

moved into an airbnb near the office since my time at sentral was up. i no longer have to endure cold showers.
