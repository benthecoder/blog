title: koreans in mexico
tags: history
date: Mar 13, 2026
---

in 1905, over 1,000 koreans boarded a british cargo ship in incheon bound for mexico. they'd answered ads for work, and ended up on henequen plantations in yucatán on four-year contracts. ([wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koreans_in_Mexico))

the ads went out in 1904 and korea's government tried to stop it. the ship landed at salina cruz on may 8, 1905 and they took a train to progreso. on the plantations, people who didn't work or didn't produce enough got beaten or jailed.

korea was under japanese rule by then and few of them had the money for a trip home, so most stayed. in 1921, 288 went on to cuba after the henequen market collapsed.

their descendants in mexico are called aenikkaeng (애니깽), after the plantations, and mostly speak only spanish.

mexico city's zona rosa has a "little seoul" now, and mérida has a museum of korean immigration. kim young-ha wrote a novel about the early community, *Black Flower* (2003).
