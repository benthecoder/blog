i noticed i have a tendency to stay quiet at the lunch table. i don't know if it's laziness to not even try to participate, but i think it's more that i idolize my insecurity and fear judgement from others, which is a lose-lose situation.

then i read [maybe social anxiety is just you failing at mind control](https://lifeimprovementschemes.substack.com/p/maybe-social-anxiety-is-just-you). his argument is that social anxiety isn't a fear of people. it's the stress of trying to do an impossible job: controlling what other people think of you.

> "social anxiety" is just the name we give to the moment-to-moment desperation of trying to accomplish any important-feeling but fundamentally impossible task.

some insights:
- approval-seeking shows up as three goals: make them like me, make them not dislike me, and make sure i'm not imposing on anyone
- the fix: drop the goal. but you can't just stop, you need a replacement goal that's fully in your control. a cop making an arrest isn't anxious about being liked, because that was never the job
- his replacement goals, in order: vulnerable honesty, kindness, selfish impulse (curiosity counts), and basic awareness of social norms. silence is allowed
  > The goal is not to be liked; the goal is to say true things, be kind, and be just a bit selfish, in that order.
- embrace social death: when the anxiety spikes, notice who you're afraid of, picture the worst case, accept it as survivable, then do what you'd do if it didn't matter
- the trap: a good reaction can restart the anxiety, because now there's approval to lose
- you can still want to be liked, you just stop trying to control it
  > Being liked by someone is a beautiful accident, to be enjoyed while it lasts.
- it's not automatic even after seven months
  > The price of rizz, I'm afraid, is constant vigilance.

it made me think of the people who share freely without hesitation. in this frame they aren't braver than me, they're running a different goal. sharing knowledge is the job, so there's nothing left over for monitoring how it lands.
