spent the day on miami beach. we started at lincoln road, a nice shopping street. i got my mom an empanada and ordered a matcha from cortadito.

![](/images/050526-img-0176.jpg)

we went into urban outfitters, then walked all the way down to the end. the street gave me south america / spain vibes.

![](/images/050526-img-0184.jpg)

![](/images/050526-img-0185.jpg)

![](/images/050526-img-0188.jpg)

we visited more souvenir shops; gifts for friends and family back home are essential.

then we went onto the beach, sat in the shade, looked for tiny seashells by our feet, and felt the water.

![my dad](/images/050526-img-0214.jpg)

we kept walking down the boardwalk and reached the art deco area. we popped into the official art deco gift shop and saw a lot of elvis presley. was he in miami? we browsed shoe palace and the adidas store, then took a lyft home.

instead of resting, my dad and i went for a swim. it's only the second time i've swum in about 9 years, so i think i'm progressing pretty fast. breathing in freestyle is tricky: water keeps going up my nose and my form isn't right, but i'll get better with time.

it was nice to swim with my dad. i felt like a kid back home again. i don't know if i'll keep swimming once i'm alone here; the gym feels a lot safer. it was cold getting out of the water.

ordered cuban food from la carreta for dinner, 60 dollars for everything:

- ropa vieja: shredded beef stewed in tomato sauce
- boliche: cuban pot roast, eye of round stuffed with chorizo
- lechón asado: roast pork marinated in mojo
- frijoles negros: black beans
- arroz amarillo: yellow rice
- yuca hervida: boiled cassava with garlic mojo
- fufú: mashed plantains
- mixed vegetables
