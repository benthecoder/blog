til:
- hebrew is a biblical term for abraham and his descendants
- israelite is the term english bibles use for the children of israel who crossed the desert after the exodus and settled in the land of israel
- jew comes from the anglicization of yehudi, someone from the kingdom of judea, where membership in the nation meant following its laws and religion
- in short
  - hebrew = ethnic marker: israelites, edomites, moabites, ammonites
  - israelite = tribal marker: judah, levi, dan, etc.
  - jew = religious marker: orthodox, reform, etc.
