  - every loop needs a fixed upper bound so its runtime is predictable

- [Earth Species Project](https://earthspecies.org/)

  - a nonprofit using ai to decode animal communication, with studies on crows, beluga whales and elephants
  - their audio-language model, naturelm-audio, was trained on animal sounds plus human speech and music
  - one study found that elephant rumbles encode information about behavior
