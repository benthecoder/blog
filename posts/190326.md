went to uhealth dermatology to get a consultation on my chest. the doctor took one look, saw the keloids and redness, and injected Triamcinolone into them. Triamcinolone is a kind of steroid that tells the overreacting repair cells in the keloid to slow down and stop piling on collagen. it also breaks down the existing pile and helps tighten the small blood vessels to make the swelling and redness go away. i was bandaged around the injection areas to avoid bleeding into my clothes.

i was prescribed Clindamycin 1% lotion (an antibiotic) and was told to get the silicone gel from amazon.
