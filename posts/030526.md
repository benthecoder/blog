today we're going for a marco island boat tour with [the dolphin explorer](https://dolphin-study.com/). a spur-of-the-moment decision i made late last night. the price is a little steep, but there's no better way to spend money than on shared experiences and adventure with family.

you're supposed to arrive 20-25 min early, and when we got there a tanned man gave us the bad news. he pulled up the radar map on his phone and told us a storm was coming. he was going to push it to 11 am, but we decided to just go for it. lots and lots of bloodthirsty mosquitoes were terrorizing us in the parking lot, and the crew shared their bug spray.

given the weather, we went straight for dickmans point and saw eastern brown pelicans, white ibises and dolphins.

![pelican at dickmans point](/images/030526-img-9957.jpg)

![our boat for the morning](/images/030526-boat.jpg)

on the way, we were told one of the houses belongs to shania twain.

we arrived at a small island, and he gave us an hour for shelling.

each of us went our separate ways to forage for shells, spreading far and wide, as if it were an intense solo competition where the one with the most shells would win a prize. it felt nice not to think about work: to feel the sand under my feet, to hunt for real things in the real world, and not obsess over technical details on a digital screen.

the guide was kind enough to help us pick out special shells. and he said we were lucky: because of the storm, no one else was around, and we had the island all to ourselves.

![the haul, sand dollars and all](/images/030526-shells.jpg)

after the tour, we went back to the airbnb to pack, then drove around the rich neighborhoods looking at mansions. last night's dinner was a letdown and we had to do better, so we picked island gypsy cafe & marina bar, which turned out pretty good.

![chicken and cilantro rice at island gypsy](/images/030526-img-0083.jpg)

claude insisted i had to try gator bites, so we stopped at joanie's blue crab cafe. the bugs there were hell, and i fled like it was the plague.

![gator bites at joanie's](/images/030526-img-0093.jpg)

we weren't satisfied until we saw alligators, so we stopped at h.p. williams roadside park, and luckily we found not one, not two, but three! the bugs were unbearable, so we didn't stay long.

![spot the gator](/images/030526-img-0097.jpg)

![gator no. 2, up close](/images/030526-img-0098.jpg)

for dinner, [kon chau](https://www.theinfatuation.com/miami/reviews/kon-chau) was closed and tropical chinese was too packed, so we had [hi pot](https://www.hipotmiami.com/) and drove through downtown.

![hi pot](/images/030526-img-0136.jpg)

all in all it was a fruitful day.
