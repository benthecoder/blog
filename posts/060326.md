do humans have special wiring for gentle touch? ([source](https://youtube.com/shorts/RrfkCslhUTM?si=UC__XDPMJhiZFYWK))
- C-tactile (CT) afferents are slow, unmyelinated nerve fibers in hairy skin
- they fire for light stroking at about 1–10 cm/s, roughly the speed you pet a cat or stroke someone's arm
- people rate that same speed as the most pleasant, and the fibres fire less for touch that's slower or faster.
- researchers call this the "affective touch" system. it seems to carry how touch feels more than where or what it is
- it's been linked to bonding, reducing stress, inhibiting pain
