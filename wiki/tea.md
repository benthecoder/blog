All true **tea** comes from one plant, *Camellia sinensis*. What makes green,
oolong, and black tea different is oxidation — how long the picked leaves are
left to react with air before drying.

Like [[Coffee]], tea's kick comes from caffeine, but it also carries L-theanine,
which is why the lift feels calmer.
