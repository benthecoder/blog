**Coffee** is brewed from the roasted seeds ("beans") of the coffee cherry. The
two species that matter commercially are Arabica (sweeter, more acidic) and
Robusta (harsher, more caffeine).

Roast level is the big lever on flavour: lighter roasts keep more of the bean's
origin character, darker roasts taste more of the roast itself.

Shares its stimulant, caffeine, with [[Tea]] — though a cup of coffee usually
carries roughly twice as much.
